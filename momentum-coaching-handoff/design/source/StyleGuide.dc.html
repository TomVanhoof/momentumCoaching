02 · Typography

Three typefaces, each with one job

Headings Aa Bricolage Grotesque · Semibold

Friendly and a bit characterful. H1 96 px, H2 54 px, H3 22–30 px, tight letter-spacing.

Personal accents Fuai Instrument Serif · Italic

Only for one or two words per heading: her name, better, stronger, amazing, momentum. Never full sentences.

Body & UI Aa Figtree · Regular / Semibold

Clear and easy to read. Body 17–19 px, labels 13 px in capitals with wide spacing.

04 · Accent — use it for
  • The main button: "Book a free intro"
  • One highlighted word in a big heading
  • Icons, numbers and words on the dark petrol background
  • Always with dark Ink text (#15232B) on top
Accent — avoid
  • As text colour on the light background — too little contrast to read
  • Large background areas or whole sections
  • White text on the accent
  • Mixing two accent colours on one page
